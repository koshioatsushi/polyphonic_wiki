# Privacy policy for using polyphonic_wiki

`polyphonic_wiki` is local-first. The public repository is only a starter kit: schemas, templates, tools, skills, and synthetic examples.

Your actual wiki should usually stay private.

## Rules for private data

- Do not publish real `inbox/`, `topics/`, `voices/`, `events/`, `logs/`, `reports/`, `archive/`, or `sources/` contents.
- Prefer paraphrases over direct quotes for P2 entries.
- Use `speaker_role` rather than real names when storing field reactions.
- Mark `privacy`, `consent`, and `sensitivity` explicitly for P2 entries.
- Do not store full transcripts containing personal information by default.
- Treat AI-mediated P1.5 entries as hypotheses, frames, objections, or questions, not factual evidence.

## Public examples

All examples in this repository should be synthetic. Do not include real customer names, interview subjects, private correspondence, undisclosed strategy, compensation data, legal records, health data, personal contact information, or raw transcripts.
